ABOUT MEKONG PACK SOLUTIONS

Mekong Pack Solutions is a simulated Cambodian packaging supplier
based in Phnom Penh. The company has operated since 2018.

This student website introduces paper bags, cardboard boxes, food
containers and protective packaging to retail customers, businesses and
potential partners.
